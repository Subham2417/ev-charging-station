# EV Charging Station Investor Website

A multi-page static investor website for an EV charging station project.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Overview
- `business.html` — Business model and customer segments
- `technology.html` — Technical concept and site selection
- `impact.html` — Environmental / ESG impact
- `financials.html` — Interactive illustrative financial calculator
- `roadmap.html` — Growth, evolution and risk management
- `investor.html` — Investor enquiry form placeholder

## Important before publishing
1. Replace `VOLTERRA EV` with your actual business name.
2. Replace the demo investor form with Formspree, Google Forms, or your own backend.
3. Replace illustrative financial assumptions with your actual site-specific numbers.
4. Add your real email/phone/contact details.
5. Validate local EV-charging, electricity, safety, tax and land-use requirements.
