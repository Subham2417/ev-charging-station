EVOLUTION

One station today. A charging network tomorrow.

Growth should be gated by measurable operating proof—not by adding sites simply for scale.

01

0–6 months • Prove the pilot

Site selection, approvals, installation, launch and KPI baseline. Validate traffic, utilization, pricing, uptime and customer behavior.

02

6–18 months • Replicate

Expand to 3–5 sites, build fleet contracts, centralize monitoring and standardize installation/service playbooks.

03

18–36 months • Regional network

Target 10+ sites through owned, leased and host partnerships. Improve brand visibility and customer retention.

04

36+ months • Energy platform

Explore intercity corridors, roaming, franchise/partner models, solar-assisted charging, battery storage and smart energy services.

Risk management

Scale with discipline.

Low utilization

Mitigation: traffic study, anchor fleets, phased capacity and demand-led site selection.

Power costs

Mitigation: tariff study, load management and commercial power planning.

Downtime

Mitigation: OEM SLA, spare parts, remote monitoring and field response.

Land / lease

Mitigation: legal due diligence and sufficiently long operating agreements.

Safety

Mitigation: engineering design, electrical protection, SOPs, training and insurance.

Policy changes

Mitigation: periodic regulatory review and flexible commercial structures.
